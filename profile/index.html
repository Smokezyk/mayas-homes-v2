Meet the team↓

— The Founder

A home, thoughtfully planned.

Born in Italy, Maja lived in Belgium and Scandinavia before settling in Cascais. Warmth, comfort and natural colours continue to shape her designs.

She founded Maya’s Homes to bring design and construction together. She develops the plans and coordinates the work with the team, so the decisions made with you carry through to the finished home.

Maja speaks seven languages and handles client communication throughout the project.

— On this coast

Based in Cascais.
Close to the work.

Based in Cascais, we work along the coastline to Lisbon. We renovate houses and apartments into homes shaped around the people who will live in them.

The team coordinates materials, installations and construction. Maja keeps you involved through agreed meetings, photographs and progress updates.
