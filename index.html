The complete journey

From property
to home.

From the first search to the final details, Maja and her team coordinate the whole process.

Already have a property? We start with the design. We also welcome individual rooms and smaller renovations.

  1. 01

    Find the right property

    Homes along the Cascais coastline, considered for their renovation potential.

  2. 02

    Design your home

    Layouts, materials and 3D designs, approved together. Design is a separate service.

  3. 03

    Agree the plan

    Scope, quotation, contract, payments and timeline, agreed before work begins.

  4. 04

    Demolition & preparation

    Site preparation and demolition, following your approved design.

  5. 05

    Build & install

    Construction and installations, with cost or timing changes approved by you.

  6. 06

    Furnish & finish

    Furniture, lighting and finishing details, sourced and installed as agreed.

  7. 07

    Welcome home

    A final review of the agreed work together, then handover.
