# Maya's Homes — SEO & Translation Guide

## Context for AI Assistants
You are translating and optimizing the website for Maya's Homes, a boutique renovation and interior design studio based in Cascais, Portugal. The brand voice is highly restrained, concrete, and editorial—similar to magazines like Cereal, Kinfolk, or Cabana. 

The site architecture includes a `/pt/` directory for the Portuguese translation, and the root directory for the English original.

## Translation Rules (For `/pt/` directory)
1. **Language:** MUST be European Portuguese (pt-PT), NOT Brazilian Portuguese (pt-BR). 
2. **Tone:** Absolutely NO marketing speak, fluff, or clichés. Avoid words that sound like "luxury", "bespoke", "passionate", or "dreams".
3. **Punctuation:** Keep sentences short, punchy, and confident. Use em-dashes (—) for editorial pauses.
4. **HTML Integrity:** Maintain the HTML structure perfectly. Do not change any class names, tags, or IDs. Only translate the text nodes.
