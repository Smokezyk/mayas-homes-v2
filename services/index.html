— How Maya’s Homes works

Four steps. One signature.

Every renovation runs the same way: a conversation, a drawing, a build, and a key.

  1. Inspection. Maja walks the property with you and notes what stays, what changes.

    01

    Week 0

    The Conversation

    A first meeting — no clipboards, no quotation. Maja walks the apartment with you, in your language, and listens. Every drawing starts here.

    “I want to know how you live before I draw a line. We keep designing all the way to the keys.”

    — Maja Milič

  2. Design. Every plan is drawn and rendered in 3D before a wall comes down.

    02

    Weeks 1–3

    The Design & Permits

    Every wall, every finish — drawn, rendered in 3D, and cleared with the council before a tool is lifted. IMPIC, AMI, and Cascais preservation permits are filed in-house. What you see on screen is what you’ll walk into.

    “We draw exactly how it’s going to be. Not a vision — a specification. Every line of it is yours.”

    — Maja Milič

  3. The build. Demolition first, then back up to the finished core.

    03

    Weeks 4–12

    The Build

    Walls moved or removed. Beams added. The studio’s craftspeople move in. Every worker is fully declared, ensuring a fiscalização never stops your build mid-project. Weekly walkthroughs, or WhatsApp updates if you’re abroad.

    “You can change a wall at any time. The build doesn’t close the design — it keeps the conversation going.”

    — Maja Milič

  4. Handover. Full inspection, snags cleared, keys to a finished home.

    04

    Final week

    The Handover

    A key on a polished countertop. The apartment is yours, ready to live in. Optional turnkey: furniture, lighting, and dressing included.

    “And we stay on the call. A bulb in year three is still our problem.”

    — Maja Milič

— Adjacent to the renovation

Before contracts.
And long after handover.

— Before

Pre-Purchase Advice.

A second pair of eyes before you sign. Maja walks the property with you — before contracts, not after — and tells you, plainly, what the renovation will cost and whether the home is worth the work, or worth the cost. Plenty of clients commission the walk and then walk away. That is fine.

A single morning. A written summary within the week. No commission, no agency relationship.

— After

Aftercare.

Twelve months of tune-ups and trade callouts after handover, included. Beyond the first year, the studio stays on the call.

We finish your home and stay near it.

— The truth

Standards, not preferences.

Maya’s Homes does not take cosmetic-only commissions, single-room paint refreshes, or rush jobs under three months. Every home is held to the same standard, and that standard takes time. Costs are quoted privately, after a site walk — every home is different, and a quote made before seeing the room is a quote made wrong.
