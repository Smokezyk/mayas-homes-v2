— How Maya’s Homes works

Your renovation, step by step.

From the property to handover, each stage belongs to the same plan. Your scope and schedule are agreed before work begins.

  1. The property and your priorities guide the brief.

    01

    Stage 1

    Property & brief

    Tell Maja how you want to live in the home and what you hope to change. The property and your priorities shape the project.

  2. See the proposed design in 3D before work begins.

    02–03

    Stages 2 & 3

    Design & agreement

    Layouts, materials and 3D designs are developed and approved with you. Before construction, you receive the detailed quotation, works contract, payment schedule and timeline.

    Design is a separate paid service. You can take the finished design to another contractor; Maja’s supervision is available for an additional fee.

    Planning the details? Read our renovation notes, including what to consider before choosing underfloor heating.

  3. The team coordinates the construction and installations.

    04–05

    Stages 4 & 5

    Preparation & construction

    The in-house team manages the work. Maja keeps you informed through site meetings, messages, photographs and progress reports suited to your needs. Changes need your approval, with any effects on price and timing confirmed in writing first.

  4. The agreed design comes together in the finished home.

    06–07

    Stages 6 & 7

    Furnishing & handover

    The team completes the agreed work and installations. Furniture, lighting and finishing details are coordinated as set out in your project scope.
