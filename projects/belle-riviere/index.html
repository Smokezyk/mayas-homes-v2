— Project Nº 03

Belle Rivière

A villa in Cascais drawn for an international family who wanted the garden and the office to feel like the same room.

Location
Cascais
Scope
Full villa renovation
Year
2025
Surface
244 m²

— The brief

A villa, redrawn around how the family actually lives.

The studio’s largest commission to date. A 244-square-metre villa with a garden the family used every day and an office the parent worked from full-time, and a brief that asked for the threshold between them to disappear. We took down a section of the south facade, rebuilt the office wall as a glazed sliding system, and laid the same floor material indoors and out. The room reads as one when both sides are open. It is two when the door closes for a call.

— The work

“The threshold is what we redrew. Same floor in, same floor out. When the glass slides back, the room is the garden.”

— Maja Milič

— Materials & finishes
