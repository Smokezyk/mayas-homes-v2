The starting point. An 80 m² top-floor T2 in Alvide, with a dated kitchen, blue-and-white tiles and a balcony overlooking the Atlantic.

The renovation. Vertical oak slats connect the living room and bedroom. A dusty rose kitchen, pale oak floors and warm textiles give the compact home a cohesive palette.
