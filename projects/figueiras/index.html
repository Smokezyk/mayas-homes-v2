— The brief

A first home. Original floors, new everything else.

A young couple’s first apartment in the Torre district, with original Portuguese terracotta floors that had to stay and almost everything around them that had to go. The renovation rebuilt walls, electrics, plumbing, and joinery, and then put the room back together around a single tonal decision — a soft mauve through the kitchen and living spaces that picks up the warmth in the terracotta without competing with it. The couple chose the colour. The studio chose what would survive next to it.

— The work

“Their colour. We chose what would live with it. The terracotta did most of the deciding for us.”

— Maja Milič
