— Project Nº 05

La Sala

A full apartment in Carcavelos, built quietly for a Portuguese actress whose name stays with the studio.

Location
Carcavelos
Scope
Full renovation
Year
2023
Surface
110 m²

— The brief

An apartment built for someone who needs to come home to quiet.

The client works in front of cameras for a living and asked the studio for a home that was the opposite of that — quiet, warm, slow. Walls came down. The kitchen was rebuilt around a single oak island. The living room was given to a fireplace and the bookshelves around it. Bedrooms were small on purpose. The largest room is the one with no furniture in it yet — a sitting room with the windows open and the rest of the apartment behind a closed door.

— The work
