— Project Nº 01

Rosa

A small apartment in Bairro Rosário, opened up to the sea wind it had been hiding from for forty years.

Location
Bairro Rosário, Cascais
Scope
Full renovation
Year
2024
Surface
92 m²

— The brief

From three closed rooms to one breathing space.

A two-bedroom apartment two streets back from the harbour, lived in by the same family for thirty years and never opened to the air. The brief was simple — make the rooms one. The work that followed was not. A load-bearing wall came out of the spine of the floor plan, structurally engineered and signed off by a Cascais council that takes its conservation district seriously. What sits there now is a single room running the depth of the apartment, sea-light at one end, kitchen at the other.

— The work

“The Rosário neighbours had not seen the back of that flat in twenty years. Opening it was the project. The rest was finishing it without ruining the proportion.”

— Maja Milič
